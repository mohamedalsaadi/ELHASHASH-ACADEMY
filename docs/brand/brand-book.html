01 · The idea

A book laid open to keep your place is an A.

Every student knows the gesture: you stop reading and put the book down open, spine up, so you don’t lose the page. Seen from the side, it is a perfect A. Alsaadi’s name holds two of them side by side, so the mark is a pair of books joined by one gold ribbon. It says you’ll pick up exactly where you left off, which is what an online academy promises.

02 · Logo system

One wordmark, one monogram, nothing else.

Every letter is drawn on the same 14-unit stroke. The spines of the A’s are rounded like a real book; every other corner is square. Only the double A carries gold, so even the full name points to the monogram.

03 · Colour

Lapis and gold, the colours of the illuminated page.

For a thousand years the most precious books in this region were painted in ground lapis lazuli and gold leaf. The palette borrows that, not a flag and not a startup gradient. Lapis is the brand; gold is earned and appears only for progress, achievements and the ribbon.

04 · Type

Arabic leads. Latin follows in the same voice.

Reem Kufi sets Arabic headlines: geometric Kufi whose straight strokes and angled joins echo the 38° A. Readex Pro sets everything else in both scripts; it was drawn as one bilingual family, so a page mixing Arabic and English never changes tone. Digits are tabular, because grades must line up.

05 · The fold

One angle runs through everything.

The 71° slope of the A’s legs is the only diagonal the brand uses: the cut corner of a card, the edge of a banner, the gold slash on a social post. Progress is shown as a row of small A’s, one per lesson, like pages turned.

PRINCIPLE 1Gold is earned.

Gold appears only for progress, completed work and achievements. A page full of gold means a student did well, never that we decorated it.

PRINCIPLE 2Show the real number.

Grades, ranks and progress come from stored results. No invented student counts, no star ratings in place of scores.

PRINCIPLE 3Calm pages, one big moment.

The 2-second intro and the ribbon are the only showpieces. Lesson and dashboard pages stay quiet so students can study.
