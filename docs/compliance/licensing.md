# Licensing: free zone, mainland and Masdar

**Short answer.** Do not pay Masdar City Free Zone, or any free zone, until it answers four questions in
writing. A free-zone registration is not an education licence, and it does not automatically let you sell to
students in mainland UAE.

## The distinction that matters

- A **free-zone company** may trade inside its zone and, under its licence, outside the UAE. Selling goods or
  services to mainland UAE customers generally needs a mainland licence, a branch, or a local distributor.
- **Education and training** are regulated separately. Depending on the emirate and the activity, approval may
  come from ADDED (Abu Dhabi Department of Economic Development), ADEK (Abu Dhabi Department of Education and
  Knowledge), KHDA (Dubai), or the free zone’s own education authority. Online tutoring is not automatically
  covered by a general “training” activity.
- **Masdar City Free Zone** is a real free zone in Abu Dhabi. Registration there gives you a free-zone licence
  for the activities it lists. It does not by itself give you the right to teach mainland students, and it is
  not an academic accreditation.

## Send these questions before paying

1. Which licensed activities cover **online one-to-one or group tutoring** for school students? Send the exact
   activity names.
2. May the company **sell to customers resident in mainland UAE**, or only outside the UAE and inside the free
   zone? If mainland sales need a branch or an additional licence, say which.
3. Does online tutoring to minors require **ADDED, ADEK or another approval**? If yes, which, and what is the
   process and fee?
4. How will the company be treated for **UAE corporate tax**? Is it a Qualifying Free Zone Person, and does
   mainland revenue break that status?
5. Does the free zone require a **No Objection Certificate** from a current licensor, or any other consent?
6. What are the **total first-year and renewal costs**, including the activity fee, office requirement, visa
   quota and any education approval?

Ask for the answers by email. A salesperson’s verbal assurance is not evidence.

## Current position

- Keep the current licence and the current payment setup.
- Do not move the business, sign a lease, or pay a registration fee on the strength of a pitch.
- If Masdar answers in writing that online tutoring to mainland students is permitted under a named activity,
  compare that written scope with the current licence before deciding.
- If the answer is “outside the UAE only”, the free zone is a poor fit for a business whose customers are in
  the UAE.

## What to keep on file

- The current trade licence, with its activity list.
- Every written answer from Masdar, ADDED or ADEK.
- The accountant’s note on corporate-tax treatment.
- The VAT registration certificate, once issued.

This note is operational guidance, not legal advice. A UAE corporate lawyer or the relevant authority should
confirm the final position.
